01

About

An online mini-conference by Unitaware: five short talks about what the speakers are working on themselves.

Topics: how AI affects thinking, what wearables actually measure, how to turn interpretability into a map you can teach from, why the safety alignment of robots depends on language, and whether Condensation is connected to the Ontology Identification Problem.

Speakers include people from JetBrains Research, ŌURA and HiveTraceLab, and independent researchers.

Format
Online, about 2.5 hours
Language
Talks in Russian, slides mostly in English
